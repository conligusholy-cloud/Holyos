Connections

What the location needs

The Compounder Machine sits on a paved surface; parking in the immediate vicinity is one of the most important factors. What the location needs:

Parking

Right next to the unit

Parking in the immediate vicinity is one of the most important factors — customers drive straight to the service.

Water

Cold water

Standard water connection, approx. 3/4". Heating is handled by the machine.

Electricity

Power connection

400 V / approx. 3×25 A. Exact load per configuration.

Drain

Sewerage

Ideally DN 110 mm. Connection to sewerage or a tank.

Connectivity

Internet

Not included — set up a local data SIM and insert it into the router the Compounder is equipped with.

Connection costs are highly individual; the average is included in the economics model.

Distribution model

Two ways to build

A Compounder builds a portfolio of assets. A Distributor helps create new Compounders.

Compounder

Acquires Compounder Machines, reinvests cashflow and scales the portfolio. One asset funds the next.

Distributor

Operates their own machines, distributes Compounder Machines and helps create new Compounders.
